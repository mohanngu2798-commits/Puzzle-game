# Pudhir privacy policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Pudhir is an offline puzzle game. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it.

## What we collect

Nothing. Pudhir does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information.

- The app does not ask for any permissions, including internet access.
- There is no account or sign-in.
- There are no ads, analytics or tracking tools in this version.

## What stays on your phone

Your game progress (levels, stars, streaks, best times and settings) is saved only on your device.
It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

## Children

Pudhir is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If a future version adds optional features such as rewarded video ads, this policy will be updated
before that version is released, and the app will ask for any consent the law requires.
